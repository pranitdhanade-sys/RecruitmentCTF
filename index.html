03 // READ BEFORE DEPLOYMENT

Rules of Engagement.

General Rules

  • Do not share flags or complete solutions with other participants.
  • Do not attack the CTF infrastructure or other participants.
  • Use only the resources and challenge files provided.
  • Do not brute-force the platform or disrupt its services.
  • Follow the instructions provided with each challenge.

Flag Format

  • All flags follow the format: VishwaCTF{...}
  • Flags are case-sensitive.
  • Submit the complete flag, including the prefix and braces.
  • Each challenge has its own unique flag.
  • Contact the organizers if you encounter a technical issue.
